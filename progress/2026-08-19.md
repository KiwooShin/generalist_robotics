# 2026-08-19 — M1 foundation, adversarial review, and a host OOM

## Host OOM (root-caused and fixed)

Three concurrent GPU sub-agents wedged the Spark and forced a reboot.

**Root cause.** JAX preallocates 75% of "GPU" memory; on unified memory that is system RAM.
One process reserved **116.5 GiB of 121 GiB** (`total_vm` 369 GiB and 146 GiB for two of the
three in the OOM report). The processes then blocked in **D-state inside the NVIDIA driver**
(`Nv04AllocWithAccessSecInfo`), where the OOM killer could not reap them — it killed
`wireplumber` instead and the host hung. Signature: `task python:NNNN blocked for more than
122 seconds`.

**Fix.** `runtime/gpu.py`; `import generalist_robotics` now sets
`XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false` and `MEM_FRACTION=0.25` → **22.1 GiB virtual, 30.4 GiB
device limit**. `gpu_lock()` makes concurrent GPU work fail fast. Operational rule adopted:
GPU sub-agents run **sequentially**, never as a parallel wave.

## Adversarial review found four defects that would not have crashed

1. **Dynamic similarity was fake** — kp/kd/damping/frictionloss unscaled, so a "similar" k=2
   robot collapsed through the floor. Fixed; similarity now holds to roundoff
   (`max|z_k/k − z_base|` 0.9346 → **1.7e-16**). The derivation **corrected the reviewer**:
   kd and `dof_damping` scale as **k^4.5**, not k^3.5.
2. **`torque_scale` was inert above ~2.2×** — bit-identical dynamics at 4, 16 and 10⁴. Now
   two-sided.
3. **The task was not scaled with the body** — foot-height targets and velocity commands stayed
   at base-robot values. Now opt-in `scale_task`, with a full audited per-key exponent table.
4. **A motionless robot counted as viable.** Viability is now a **Froude number** (dimensionless,
   similarity-invariant, `Fr = v²/gL`, threshold 0.01 ≈ 40–55% of the commanded average).

Two further silent bugs surfaced during the fixes:
- **go1 / go1_rough / spot / op3 overwrite Kp/Kd in `base.py` before `mjx.put_model`**, so the
  morph's gains were being discarded on 4 of 12 robots.
- Joystick commands are symmetric about zero, so a perfect policy averages ≈0 forward velocity;
  `mean_forward_speed` added because any positive bar on the signed mean is always-false.
