**Key findings**
- The architecture has converged industry-wide: VLM backbone + flow/diffusion action expert +
  action chunking.
- Three competing answers to the embodiment gap: pool-and-scale, align-explicitly, or
  erase-the-gap-in-the-data.
- The demo budget to onboard a new robot fell ~1,000 (2023) → ~100 (2025) → <200 (2026).
- **Adaptation efficiency is a property of the pre-trained prior, not the fine-tuning
  procedure** — the same <200-demo recipe gave 6.7% vs 53.3% on SO-101 across two model
  generations.
- Open gap: morphology/kinematic-graph encoding is mature in locomotion RL and nearly absent
  from manipulation VLAs.
